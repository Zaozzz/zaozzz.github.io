Biography

about me

Hi, I am Xu Wang, an undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Shandong Jianzhu University (Class of 2023). I also serve as the Psychological Committee Member of my class and lead a campus entrepreneurship team. My research interests lie in Natural Language Processing and Computer Vision, with a focus on controllable text generation, object detection, and image classification.

I have published 20+ papers in top venues and journals such as AAAI, ICASSP, IP&M and OLT. My controllable paragraph-generation work was praised by renowned science-fiction writers (e.g., Yan Wu). The ECG benchmark I proposed was listed by the MiniMind (50k+ GitHub stars) project as an inspiring research outcome. I also proposed frameworks for oracle-bone inscription deduplication and fragment stitching, which won 2nd and 3rd place in the Tencent SSV AI+Oracle competition.

Over the past year, I participated in two Provincial Undergraduate Innovation & Entrepreneurship Training Program projects. I received the CNCC 2024 Student Travel Award, the ICASSP 2026 Student Travel Award, National Gold Award in the "ChuangQingChun" 2025, the Grand Prize (Shandong Province) in the "Challenge Cup", National First Prize in the Lanqiao Cup 2025, and National First Prize in the CRAIC 2025, among 100+ awards.

I am invited to serve as a reviewer for conferences such as ICME, AVSS and BIBE, and journals such as TKDE. I am also a student member of AAAI, CCF, SDCAI. Beyond academic research, I am also a recognized NetEase Cloud Music playlist curator and music-review writer. My playlists have attracted over 3 million plays and tens of thousands of saves, while my published music comments have received hundreds of thousands of likes. Several of my playlists have entered the NetEase Cloud Music Soaring Playlist Chart Top 20, and my comments have repeatedly been featured on the platform's hot-comment wall. I also founded the public account ICCV (I Code Computer Vision), where I have published dozens of paper-sharing articles and accumulated tens of thousands of reads.
