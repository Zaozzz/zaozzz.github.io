03 / What comes next

Proactive help, with boundaries you control.

Our next direction is a personal AI for people carrying demanding responsibilities: preparing information, planning time, and handling authorized routine work, while leaving important decisions with you.

CONCEPT / IN EXPLORATION
01

Permission before action.

Reading information does not grant permission to act. Sensitive work needs explicit data and action boundaries.

02

Evidence before advice.

Show the sources, alternatives, and unknowns. Keep the final judgment with the person responsible.

03

Room for life.

Rest and play remain a choice. Avoid reminders or reward systems that turn relaxation into another obligation.
