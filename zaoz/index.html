01 / About

Ideas into meaningful things.

ZAOZ is an independent technology and creative brand founded by Zaozzz.

Rooted in artificial intelligence, research, and design, ZAOZ explores ideas and turns them into meaningful products.

We believe in independent thinking, decisive action, and long-term value creation.

ZAOZ 是由 Zaozzz 发起的独立科技与创意品牌。

以人工智能为起点，连接科研、技术、设计与产品创造。

我们相信独立思考、主动行动和长期积累。让能力转化为作品，让作品积累价值，让价值随时间持续增长。

02 / Philosophy

A way of working.

Independent Thinking.

Start with questions. Make considered judgments. Take responsibility for the direction.

从问题出发，独立判断，主动行动。

Continuous Creation.

Use AI to extend capability. Learn through research, craft, and the steady practice of building.

用 AI 拓展能力，在研究与创造中持续精进。

Long-Term Value.

Build knowledge, useful work, and trust over time. Let each creation become a foundation for the next.

积累知识、作品与信誉，让每次创造成为下一步的基础。
